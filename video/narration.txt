ShiftSwap is a printable swap board for small retail and food teams that still use paper.

The week is local. Every shift shows time, role, coverage and workers.

Noor offers her Monday morning shift and proposes Jamie's Wednesday opening. The checker refuses it because her Tuesday close leaves only eight hours of rest.

The policy panel names the exact rule and threshold. In this case, rest gap R1 requires ten hours, so the failure is inspectable.

Maya then offers Wednesday close and trades with Alex on Thursday opening. The rest gap, weekly hours, role match and double booking rules all pass.

The accept button republishes revision two, with both assignments changed and the printable payload refreshed.

ShiftSwap stays offline and honest. It prints the week, explains constraints, and deliberately leaves payroll, exports and accounts outside the prototype.
